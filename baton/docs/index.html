When someone leaves, the reasons shouldn’t.

Baton turns a team’s scattered repositories, notes, tasks and chats into a handover for whoever takes over. Every claim shows its source, gaps are marked instead of guessed, and people only see what they’re cleared to see.

From scattered activity to a handover you can approve

Five steps, with a person in charge at the point that matters.

  1. ConnectBring in authorised sources. Each one gets an access level, and sensitive details are redacted.
  2. ReconstructPull out decisions, owners, risks, dependencies, open questions and next actions.
  3. VerifyCheck each claim against its cited text and label how well it is supported.
  4. ReviewThe outgoing member answers gap questions. A reviewer edits, rejects and approves.
  5. Hand overThe newcomer gets a view for their role, a first-week plan, and an Acknowledge button.

Built so you can tell what is known and what is not

Baton never presents a guess as organisational fact.

Every claim has a source

Click a statement and see the exact passage behind it. If there is no source, Baton says so.

Gaps become questions

Missing reasons and conflicting documents turn into specific questions for the person who is leaving.

Permissions come first

Restricted material is filtered out before a summary is generated, so it cannot leak into someone else’s view.
